Leevo Music — Lightweight Offline Player, Clean & Undisturbed Listening

We built Leevo Music to bring back pure, comfortable music listening! Unlike many players flooded with constant pop-ups, interruptions and full-screen ads during playback — we keep it respectful, lightweight and focused on your music.

Core Highlights & Ad Promise

  • ✅ Fully offline playback: play saved music files stored on your phone anytime, no internet required
  • ✅ Minimal ad design: ONLY one small banner ad on the home page — NO pop-ups, NO interruptions, NO full-screen ads inside player, playlists, folders or settings
  • ✅ Lightweight & smooth: takes little storage & memory, runs fast even on older phones, simple intuitive controls
  • ✅ Practical daily features: create custom playlists, adjust equalizer, sleep timer, quick track management — all usable without disruption
