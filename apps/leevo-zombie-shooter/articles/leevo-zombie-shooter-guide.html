What Is Leevo Zombie Shooter?

Leevo Zombie Shooter is a fast-paced mobile action shooting survival game focused on quick reflexes, precise aiming and non-stop zombie combat.

Fight through continuous waves of incoming zombies—from slow common walkers to fast, dangerous special variants—each round grows harder and more thrilling.

Collect coins, unlock and upgrade powerful firearms, unlock special abilities, defend your position and see how long you can survive the undead siege.

Exciting Core Gameplay

Simple tap/aim controls make shooting easy to pick up instantly—perfect for quick play sessions while delivering intense action moments.

Survive endless progressive waves: defeat every zombie in a wave to advance, face bigger crowds and stronger elite zombies as you go.

Earn rewards for every kill and wave cleared—spend earnings to unlock better guns, boost firepower, reload speed, ammo capacity and unlock special blast/rapid-fire skills.

Weapons & Power-Ups to Crush Zombies

Start with a reliable basic pistol, then unlock an arsenal of distinct weapons suited for different combat situations:

  • Fast SMG — high fire rate, great for crowds of fast weak zombies
  • Shotgun — wide blast spread, devastating close-range damage
  • Assault Rifle — balanced damage, speed & ammo for steady long survival
  • Sniper Rifle — precise high single-shot damage, take down tough elites from afar
  • Heavy Machine Gun — massive continuous firepower to hold back overwhelming hordes

Temporary power-ups also spawn mid-battle: instant rapid fire, explosive rounds, shield protection or ammo refills to turn desperate moments into glorious victories.

Top Survival & Shooting Tips

1. Prioritize Fast & Dangerous Zombies First

Don't only shoot slow walkers—take out runners, jumpers or explosive zombies quickly before they rush close and overwhelm you.

2. Upgrade Smartly, Don't Save Too Long

Balance firepower, reload speed and ammo capacity early—stronger guns let you clear waves faster and earn more coins to keep growing stronger.

3. Keep Moving & Watch All Directions

Standing still makes you an easy target—shift position constantly, create safe shooting angles and watch corners for enemies sneaking up behind.

4. Grab Power-Ups Immediately

Don't ignore glowing items—short boosts can save you during overwhelming crowds or boss fights and push your survival record much further.
