Leevo Learn: PSLE Math — Daily Practice & Exam Familiarity Guide

Designed specially for Singapore Primary 6 students! This app focuses on helping learners get fully familiar with standard PSLE Mathematics question types, core knowledge points, common problem-solving methods and exam answering habits — build solid foundation through regular daily learning instead of last-minute cramming.

Core Learning & Revision Advantages

  • ✅ Get familiar with all PSLE Math question categories: Numbers, Algebra, Geometry, Measurement, Statistics & Word Problems
  • ✅ Daily small-volume practice — easy to stick to, avoid burnout, keep math thinking active every day
  • ✅ Mistake review & consolidation — clearly see weak areas, correct misconceptions, avoid repeating errors
  • ✅ Build exam confidence gradually — know what to expect, master answering pace & skills before the real PSLE

Steady progress day by day

Whether it is basic concept strengthening, skillful calculation speed-up or difficult problem thinking training — consistent daily practice helps Primary 6 students turn scattered knowledge into a complete system, improve problem-solving speed & accuracy, and achieve excellent results in Singapore PSLE Mathematics.
