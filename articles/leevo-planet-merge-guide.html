Leevo Planet Merge — Grow Planets, Explore the Solar System, Relax Your Mind

A gentle, space-themed puzzle game built for quiet little moments of rest! Inspired by our beautiful solar system: start with tiny scattered asteroids, let them collide and merge into small rocky planets, then keep combining to grow bigger, more magnificent planets step by step — no rush, no pressure, just satisfying merging visuals & peaceful space atmosphere.

Gameplay & Highlights

  • ✅ Solar system theme: follow the growth path from tiny asteroids → terrestrial planets → larger planets close to the real solar system
  • ✅ Simple intuitive merge rule: bring two same-level planets together to combine into one larger, higher-level planet
  • ✅ Low-pressure calm pace: no countdown panic, no difficult reflex demand — pick up anytime, play for 5 minutes to calm down, put down easily
  • ✅ Beautiful visual & satisfying feedback: smooth merging animation, soft cosmic colors — help ease tired mind & release daily stress

Perfect for short pleasant breaks

Between work/study, while waiting for a bus, before bed — let yourself drift into quiet space for a little while, watch planets grow slowly, breathe easier, feel lighter & happier after a quick relaxing session.
