Leevo Push Box — Classic Logic Puzzle, Think Carefully & Clear Levels

Leevo Push Box is a timeless brain-teasing puzzle game loved by players of all ages! Simple core rule: control the character to push boxes one by one onto the glowing marked target positions — once all boxes are in place, you clear the level! Sounds easy, but needs careful planning, avoid pushing into dead corners or blocking paths.

Game & Brain Benefits

  • ✅ Easy to start: simple control, clear objective — pick up quickly without complicated tutorials
  • ✅ Step-by-step challenge: levels go from easy warm-up to tricky hard puzzles — keep thinking, never get boring fast
  • ✅ Exercise key abilities: train logical reasoning, space layout & forward planning — good brain workout for kids & adults alike
  • ✅ Great time-killer: play a few rounds during breaks, waiting time or before bed — short sessions, full sense of achievement when clearing a level

Small tips to play better

Think ahead before pushing — do not rush! Observe the whole map first, plan which target to finish first, avoid pushing boxes into corners you can’t pull them out from. Take your time, try different ideas, reset & rethink freely — thinking process itself is the fun!
